# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

> "Friends don't lie." - Eleven

## About the Series
**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror series on Netflix set in the 1980s in a small town called Hawkins. It starts when a young boy goes missing and his friends uncover secret government experiments and a parallel dimension called the *Upside Down*.


### Main Characters
* **Eleven** - A girl with psychokinetic abilities who escaped from Hawkins Lab.
* **Mike Wheeler** - One of the main kids who helps Eleven and leads the group.
* **Jim Hopper** - The chief of police in Hawkins who investigates the strange events.
* **Dustin Henderson** - The comic relief of the group who is always curious about science.

### Reasons Why I Like It
1. **80s Nostalgia** - The soundtrack, outfits, and retro vibe are really cool.
2. **Great Cast** - The chemistry between the characters feels genuine and fun to watch.
3. **Good Mix of Genres** - It balances suspense, mystery, and humor well.
